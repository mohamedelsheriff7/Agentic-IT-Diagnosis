## Fixed

- Saved investigations can introduce a previously absent canonical shared-service hypothesis when recorded user answers or diagnostic checks support it. Retrieval alone cannot introduce it through this recovery path.
- Supporting and contradictory evidence IDs are preserved. Repeated resume does not duplicate candidates or require another answer.
- Supported shared-service hypotheses take priority over individual diagnosis. A prior individual conclusion is cleared when shared-service screening becomes necessary.
- ESCALATE replies prioritize an explicit Supervisor outage-screening recommendation. Older checkpoints containing peer failure are also explained correctly, even if their old candidate lists were incomplete.
- Both the local client error and colleagues' failures remain visible. The agent does not claim that an outage is confirmed, an assignment occurred, or a global incident was created.

The knowledge articles and approval flags were not changed. Diagnostic guidance remains bundled in the import package; the existing `isys_it_backend` team API-key connection is still required.
